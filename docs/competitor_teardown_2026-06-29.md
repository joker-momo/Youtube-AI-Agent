## Visual archetypes (keyframe evidence)

1. **Static AI talking-head** (Dra. Sofía, Doctor Movimiento static ones): ONE
   AI-generated "doctor" image for the whole 22–29 min video (shots=1, motion
   very_static). Office set, white coat, anatomy poster, diploma. Overlays:
   persona name top-left, red **SUSCRÍBETE** button top-right, circular avatar
   badge bottom-right. Lowest production possible.
2. **AI talking-head + inserts** (Enfermera Ana, Cardiogeriatra Omar): a presenter
   (Black nurse / clinician) intercut with **illustrations** (painted hand holding
   a clove) or **full infographic data-cards** ("PROTOCOLO 2 … 40% de pacientes …"
   with a curve graph + "Qué hacer" box). Enfermera Ana = the top performer (450k).
3. **Medical stock animation + subtitles** (Dr. Miguel / gases): 3D anatomy clips
   (glowing intestines x-ray) with burned-in 2-line subtitles; 71 shots = many
   stock clips, no face.

Common to almost all: **persistent SUBSCRIBE + persona badge overlays** and
**word-by-word highlighted burned-in captions** (e.g. "NADA Y PUEDE" with the
active word highlighted) — a retention device.

- **Hook** = curiosity-gap + authority secret + "nadie te lo dice": "Hay algo que
  nadie le dijo cuando le entregaron esa receta", "¿Sabía usted que la mayoría de
  los ataques al corazón…", "La mayoría cree que … pero …".
- **Authority persona** is the spine: fake/branded credentials ("Soy el doctor
  Movimiento, 30 años en medicina", "Enfermera Ana, 8 años de experiencia
  clínica"). ⚠️ pseudo-authority — do NOT copy.
- **Numbers everywhere** (10–47/video): %, ages, mg, minutes, "3% de masa muscular
  cada año".
- **CTA** = persona signoff + warmth ("Cuídese mucho, un abrazo, nos vemos en el
  próximo").

## Voice / audio / pacing

- **WPM 170–220** — much faster than our Elena (~116–145). Fast AI delivery.
- **F0** mix: male AI (135–152) and female AI (195–267). Multiple synthetic voices.
- **LUFS −18 to −23** — quiet, voiceover-style (vs −14 broadcast / −12 music).
- Pacing: from 0 cuts (static image) to 4.9 cuts/min — all SLOWER visually than a
  real edited video; production is minimal.

## Title / description / tags

- Titles: curiosity + number + authority + contrarian: "Agrega 1 clavo en el
  café… lo que sucede 10 minutos después es sorprendente", "La presión ideal …
  (No es 12 por 8)", "Cardiólogo advierte el silencioso error matutino…".
- Descriptions: restate the hook + persona credential + the promise.
- Tags: keyword-stuffed (17–33) on established channels; newer channels (Dra.
  Sofía, Dr. Miguel) ship with **0 tags** yet still get 20–133k views → title +
  thumbnail + hook matter more than tags here.

## What this means for OUR channel (Vida Plena 45+)

We use REAL Pexels stock video + the Elena presenter + graphic cards — **already
higher production** than most of this set (several are a single AI image). So our
gap is NOT visual polish.

Where we lag (fixable in script/SEO, safely):
1. **Hook curiosity-gap** — they open with a secret/number/contrarian; we open
   with pain+reframe (good, but add the curiosity/number device).
2. **Number density** — they pack 10–47 specifics; we average ~1. (Already a
   pending script-prompt change.)
3. **Open-loops / retention** — they forward-reference ("el secreto está al
   final"); we do ~0.
4. **Burned-in word-highlight captions** — common retention device; check if our
   render does this.
5. **Persistent SUBSCRIBE + presenter badge overlay** — they keep it on-screen.
6. **Title formula** — curiosity + number + contrarian + "nadie te dice".

Where we are STRONGER (keep):
- Real stock footage > single AI image.
- Short TTS-friendly sentences; intimate "tú"; honest framing.
- We must NOT adopt their fake authority personas / pseudo-medical claims
  (prime directive + medical_safety_checker). Learn the STRUCTURE, not the claims.

Open question to test, not assume: **WPM**. They run 170–220; our Elena ~116. A
faster pace MAY lift retention, but clarity for 45+ is a feature. Treat as an A/B,
not a mandate.
